# Extended demo video of the Sudoku app, for the website (site/). Steps 1 to 7 are the same as
# video/demo.txt. Then the player solves the puzzle to the end, as in a real game: with pencil marks,
# pauses to think, two more incorrect numbers (one is erased, one is undone) and the completion.
# Make it with:
#   cargo run --release --bin video -- --no-gif video/demo-full.txt video/demo-full.mp4
# Cells are rRcC, with rows and columns from 1 to 9. The commands are in src/bin/video.rs.
# Each write has a different writer, thus no two numbers have the same handwriting.

fps 30
puzzle seed 7 mode 0          # Easy. The same puzzle each time.
hand right                    # the menu is at the left edge

wait 1.5                      # show the new board

# 1. Pencil marks in the bottom-middle box (true candidates only).
mark r7c4 1 8
mark r8c5 2 9
mark r9c4 1 4 8
wait 1.0

# 2. Two obvious numbers in the top-right box (3 and 9 are missing).
write r1c8 3 writer 1         # 9 is already in column 8, thus r1c8 is 3
write r2c9 9 writer 2
wait 1.0

# 3. An incorrect number. r3c4 must be 5 (the last empty cell in row 3).
write r3c4 9 writer 7         # a different 9 (round loop). It clashes with r3c6 and r4c4: the cells are red
wait 2.0

# 4. Change the clash color.
tap menu
wait 0.8
tap colors                    # opens the Cell colors panel
wait 1.0
tap color clashing orange     # the panel covers the clashing cells: they change to orange below it
wait 1.0
tap colors                    # closes the panel: the board shows the orange cells
wait 3.0
tap menu                      # closes the menu
wait 1.0

# 5. Erase the incorrect number.
erase r3c4
wait 1.0

# 6. More pencil marks in the middle-left box.
mark r4c2 2 5
mark r5c2 5
mark r6c1 1
wait 1.0

# 7. The last obvious number: r5c2 can only be 5.
write r5c2 5 writer 3         # automatic hiding hides the 5 in r4c2
wait 2.5

# The rest of the game. Above, the player wrote fast. Now the player thinks between the steps.

# 8. The correct number in row 3, then column 1 (1 and 8 are missing).
write r3c4 5 writer 4
wait 1.2
write r6c1 1 writer 5         # row 6 has no 1, and r6c1 has the pencil mark 1
write r1c1 8 writer 6
wait 1.5

# 9. The top-left box: 6 and 9 are missing.
mark r1c2 6 9
mark r2c2 6                   # row 2 has a 9 (r2c9): a tap on the 9 has no effect
wait 2.0
write r2c2 6 writer 8         # automatic hiding hides the 6 in r1c2
wait 0.6
write r1c2 9 writer 9
wait 1.5

# 10. Row 1: 4 and 6 are missing. A guess that is not correct.
write r1c4 4 writer 10        # no clash yet
write r1c6 6 writer 11        # clashes with the 6 in r7c6 (column 6)
wait 2.0
tap menu
wait 0.6
tap undo                      # removes the 6
wait 0.6
tap undo                      # removes the 4
wait 0.6
tap menu
wait 0.8
write r1c6 4 writer 12
write r1c4 6 writer 13
wait 1.5

# 11. The top-middle box: 2, 3 and 8 are missing.
write r2c4 8 writer 14        # automatic hiding hides the 8 in r7c4 and r9c4
wait 1.0
mark r2c5 2                   # column 5 has a 3 (r6c5): the player does not tap it
mark r2c6 2 3
wait 1.5
write r2c5 2 writer 15        # automatic hiding hides the 2 in r2c6
wait 0.5
write r2c6 3 writer 16
wait 1.5

# 12. Column 4: r7c4 shows only 1 now.
write r7c4 1 writer 17        # automatic hiding hides the 1 in r9c4
wait 0.5
write r9c4 4 writer 18
wait 1.5

# 13. The center box: 1, 4, 5 and 6 are missing.
mark r4c5 6
mark r4c6 1 5
mark r5c5 4
mark r5c6 1
wait 1.2
write r5c6 1 writer 19        # automatic hiding hides the 1 in r4c6
write r4c6 5 writer 20
write r4c5 6 writer 21
write r5c5 4 writer 22
wait 1.5

# 14. Rows 4 and 5.
write r4c2 2 writer 23
write r4c9 1 writer 24
wait 1.0
write r5c9 8 writer 25        # incorrect: column 9 has an 8 (r8c9)
wait 2.0
erase r5c9
wait 0.8
write r5c9 7 writer 26
write r5c7 8 writer 27
wait 1.5

# 15. Row 6: 5 and 6 are missing.
write r6c9 5 writer 28
write r6c8 6 writer 29
wait 1.5

# 16. Row 7 and column 2.
mark r7c2 8                   # column 2 has a 9 (r1c2): the player does not tap it
mark r9c2 7 8
wait 1.8
write r7c2 8 writer 30        # automatic hiding hides the 8 in r9c2
write r9c2 7 writer 31
write r7c3 2 writer 32
write r7c7 9 writer 33
wait 1.5

# 17. Column 7: 1 and 6 are missing.
mark r8c7 6
mark r9c7 1                   # a peer has a 6: the player does not tap it
wait 1.0
write r8c7 6 writer 34
write r9c7 1 writer 35
wait 1.2

# 18. Row 8: r8c5 has the marks 2 and 9 from step 1. Column 5 has a 2, thus it is 9.
write r8c5 9 writer 36
write r8c8 2 writer 37
wait 1.5

# 19. The last row.
write r9c5 8 writer 38
write r9c6 2 writer 39
write r9c8 5 writer 40
wait 1.0
write r9c9 3 writer 41        # the last number
wait 5.0                      # the board shows "Completed in m:ss"
